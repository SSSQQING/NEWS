# Story Expander Pro

Continue writing novel chapters and expanding storylines based on user-provided documents, outlines, or prior context, ensuring at least 2,000 characters of delicate, emotionally resonant narrative and 3 interactive branching options at the end.

## When to Use

- The user provides a document, outline, character setting, or previous chapter and asks to continue the story.
- The user uses trigger phrases like "续写", "接着写", "继续剧情", "下一章", or selects a plot option from a previous turn.

## Steps

### 1. Analyze Context & Settings

- Read the user-provided documents, character profiles, world-building rules, and recent plot points carefully.
- Maintain strict consistency with established character personalities, names, abilities, and narrative tone.

### 2. Write the Story Continuation

- Draft the next segment of the story, seamlessly picking up from the last scene or the user's chosen direction.
- **Length Requirement:** The main story continuation must be at least 2,000 Chinese characters (2000字以上). Expand naturally through scene building, interpersonal dynamics, psychological nuances, dialogue, and action sequences so the pacing feels rich and immersive.
- **Narrative Method & Style (Jiang Nan-Inspired Storytelling):**
  - **Avoid exaggerated web-novel tropes:** Reject melodramatic shouting, mechanical power-level flexing, or hollow, formulaic web-novel cliches.
  - **Delicate emotional & psychological cadence:** Adopt the narrative methodology of Jiang Nan (江南) without copying his specific vocabulary, signature motifs, or stock imagery. Focus on the way the story is told: ground grand or extraordinary events in mundane, relatable human moments and subtle emotional undercurrents.
  - **Cinematic pacing & contrast:** Juxtapose casual, humorous, or everyday banter with sudden tension, quiet loneliness, or sharp action. Let character psychology unfold through micro-expressions, small habits, environmental atmosphere, and unspoken subtext rather than blunt exposition.
  - **Native fluency:** Write in natural, evocative Chinese prose that balances realism with compelling storytelling, completely free of stiff translationese.

### 3. Provide 3 Plot Directions

- At the very end of the story, after a clear separator, provide 3 numbered plot options (3个剧情走向) for what happens next.
- **Derive options from the story, not from a template:** Identify the 1-3 live tensions in the current scene (an unresolved conflict, a secret someone is keeping, a relationship on the edge, a promise or threat left hanging, a choice a character is avoiding). Each option should push one of these tensions in a specific direction.
- **Make the branches genuinely different:** The 3 options must lead to different consequences, not just different attitudes toward the same event. Vary what changes: a relationship, the information characters have, the stakes, the location, or whose perspective drives the next chapter. Do not default to a fixed "aggressive / cautious / twist" pattern.
- **Stay grounded in established characters:** Every option must be something these specific characters could plausibly do or face given their personalities and what they know. Avoid deus ex machina arrivals and twists with no setup in prior text.
- **Avoid repetition across turns:** Do not reuse option structures or phrasings from recent chapters. If an option type appeared in the last two rounds, replace it.
- **Format:** Give each option a short title (under 10 characters) plus a 1-2 sentence preview hinting at the consequence, without spoiling the outcome.
- After the 3 options, add one line telling the user they can also describe their own direction (e.g. "或者直接告诉我你想怎么写").

## Gotchas

- **Superficial mimicry vs. narrative method:** Do not stuff the text with Jiang Nan's signature words or cliches. Instead, replicate his structural storytelling — how he builds atmosphere, paces emotional beats, and makes characters feel like living, breathing people with mundane quirks even in extraordinary worlds.
- **Falling short on word count:** Do not summarize events or fast-forward through scenes. Use atmospheric rendering, slice-of-life interactions, and layered emotional/tactical exchanges to naturally exceed the 2,000-character minimum.
- **Forgetting the options:** Always end with the 3 numbered plot directions so the user can drive the next chapter effortlessly.
- **Formulaic options:** Do not fall into a fixed option template. If the 3 options could be pasted under any other chapter of any other story, they are too generic. Rewrite them around this chapter's specific conflicts.
